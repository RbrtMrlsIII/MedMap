# MedMap Skills

Skills are reusable execution procedures across backend, frontend, spatial, operations, commerce, accessibility, verification, and handover work.

Each Skill answers:

```text
what it is for
what it is based on
what to do
what not to do
how to verify
how to recover
what evidence to leave
what constitutes exit
```

The Masterplan controls chronological order. Skill numbers identify reusable development roots; they do not override the Masterplan.
