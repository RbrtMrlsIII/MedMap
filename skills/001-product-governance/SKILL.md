# Skill 001 — Product Governance

## Purpose
Reconcile product meaning and document wiring before implementation changes.
## Based on
`PRODUCT_LAW.md`, `POLICY.md`, `MASTERPLAN.md`, current handover, accepted evidence.
## Do
Identify authority; locate the exact owning document/section; reconcile dependencies; record unresolved questions; update path references.
## Do not
Solve product conflicts in code; duplicate Product Law inside Policy; create Skills that grant permissions.
## Verification
Cross-reference paths, headings, and responsibility boundaries.
## Evidence
Reconciliation note + file/path audit.
## Exit criteria
No material authority/path conflict remains for the slice.
